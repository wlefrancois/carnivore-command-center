How To Use The Dashboard

The goal is not perfection. The goal is learning what helps, what hurts, and how quickly you can get back on track.

1

Start Your Daily Check-In

Open the check-in page and enter your daily signals. It only takes about 60 seconds and gives CCC the data it needs to build your score.

2

Enter Sleep, Mood, Stress, And Recovery

These signals help explain why some days feel strong and others feel off. Sleep, stress, soreness, digestion, and energy all affect readiness.

3

Choose Your Nutrition Quality

Pick the closest match: Strict Carnivore, Animal-Based, Low Carb, or High Carb. This keeps the free version simple while Pro can later unlock deeper macro and meal tracking.

4

Save Your Progress

Saving your check-in adds XP, updates your streak, and creates a history you can compare over time. One day does not define you — returning does.

5

Review Your Dashboard

Your dashboard shows readiness, trends, recent entries, and momentum. Use it to see whether your choices are helping or making consistency harder.

6

Watch Patterns Over Time

If carbs or sugar bring back cravings, track it. If better sleep improves energy, track it. CCC helps you learn your body instead of guessing.
